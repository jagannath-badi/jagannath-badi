### About Me

I am a second-year undergraduate engineering student focused on the intersection of physical hardware, analog circuits, and software tooling. My academic focus centers on first-principles circuit analysis, operational amplifier feedback networks, and signals & systems.

Alongside coursework, I prototype with embedded microcontrollers like the ESP32, interfacing sensors with digital logic and writing structured system documentation.
