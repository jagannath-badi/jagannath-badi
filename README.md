### About Me

I am a second-year undergraduate student in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering at **Indira Gandhi Institute of Technology (IGIT), Sarang, Odisha**.

My academic coursework covers circuit analysis, analog and digital electronics, Network Theory, and Signals and Systems. I have been actively studying operational-amplifier feedback networks, RC and Wien bridge oscillators, power amplifier classifications, Fourier transforms, convolution, and Z-transforms.

I am interested in how fundamental circuit principles connect with embedded hardware and software. My hands-on foundations include ESP32 microcontroller interfacing, sensor integration, breadboard prototyping, and digital multimeter (DMM) measurements. I use C/C++ and Python in my technical studies, alongside developing and documenting engineering systems using Astro, Supabase, and Git.
